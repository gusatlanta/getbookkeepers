Every number on this page comes from Staff Financial Group's [2026 Salary Guide](https://www.stafffinancial.com/salary-guide.html) ([PDF](https://www.stafffinancial.com/downloads/Staff-Financial-Group-Salary-Guide-2026-Accounting-Finance.pdf)). The guide is built from 1,447 Staff Financial Group and NorthPoint Search Group candidate questionnaires and job applications from January 1 to October 2, 2026, plus U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics data. These figures are what candidates ask to be paid. They are not offers, and they are not what an employer pays Staff Financial Group.

## What bookkeeping and accounting candidates ask for

Desired base salary, before bonus and benefits. The middle half is the 25th to 75th percentile of answers, rounded to $500.

| Title | Answers | Middle half | Typical ask (median) | Lowest they would take |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| AP / AR / Billing | 55 | $60K to $75K | $67K | not reported |
| Payroll | 9 | $65K to $76K | $70K | $62.5K |
| Bookkeeper | 28 | $65K to $80K | $70K | $60K |
| Staff Accountant | 113 | $65K to $80K | $70K | $70K |
| Accounting Specialist / Associate | 61 | $65K to $85K | $75K | $70K |
| Senior Accountant | 105 | $85K to $100K | $95K | $87.5K |
| Accounting Manager / Supervisor | 81 | $105K to $160K | $130K | $118K |

The guide's takeaway: the lowest salary people say they would accept sits close to what they ask for, so there is not much room to come in low. Titles with fewer than 5 answers are left out. About 35% of candidates who gave a location are in Georgia; the rest are spread across the U.S.

## Metro Atlanta government pay data

Median annual pay in the Atlanta-Sandy Springs-Roswell metro area, May 2025, from the BLS Occupational Employment and Wage Statistics survey, as reported in the guide. These include every experience level, so they can run lower than what experienced candidates ask for.

| BLS occupation | Median annual pay, metro Atlanta |
|---|---|
| Bookkeeping, accounting, and auditing clerks | $51,170 |
| Payroll and timekeeping clerks | $62,710 |
| Accountants and auditors | $85,820 |

## How to use these numbers

- **Pay to the ask.** The guide's first recommendation is to put the middle-half range for the role in your job post, because candidates' floor is close to their ask.
- **Match the title to the work.** A [full-charge bookkeeper](/roles/full-charge-bookkeeper/) who owns the close is a different hire than a bookkeeping clerk. The BLS clerk median covers every level, which is one reason it sits below the candidate ask.
- **Sell the path, not just the pay.** In the guide, 35% of 620 candidates said they are looking for growth or a next step, and only 4% named pay as the reason.
- **Offer hybrid if you can.** 84% of 736 candidates would work hybrid, 65% would work fully in the office, and only 9% will only work remote.

## Systems bookkeeping candidates know

Accounting and payroll systems named by 820 candidates in the guide (many know more than one): QuickBooks 60%, Oracle 43%, SAP 41%, Microsoft Dynamics 30%, NetSuite 30%, Sage 100, 300, or X3 27%, Workday 23%, ADP 22%, and Sage Intacct 19%. If you run something less common, ask about it in your screening questions.

## How long people stay

From BLS data in the guide: median years with their current employer, January 2026, was 4.1 years for office and administrative support (which includes AP, payroll, and bookkeeping) and 4.6 years for business and financial operations (which includes accountants). Turnover is predictable, so the guide recommends keeping a short list of ready people behind every seat.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the typical salary ask for a bookkeeper in 2026?

In Staff Financial Group's 2026 Salary Guide, the median desired base salary for bookkeepers was $70K, with a middle half of $65K to $80K, from 28 answers.

### What do AP and AR clerks ask to be paid?

The median ask for AP, AR, and billing roles was $67K, with a middle half of $60K to $75K, from 55 answers.

### What is the BLS median pay for bookkeepers in metro Atlanta?

$51,170 for bookkeeping, accounting, and auditing clerks in the Atlanta-Sandy Springs-Roswell area, May 2025. That figure covers every experience level.

### Is this what I would pay Staff Financial Group?

No. They are what candidates ask to be paid and what the BLS reports, not what employers pay Staff Financial Group.
