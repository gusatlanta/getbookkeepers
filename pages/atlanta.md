## A local firm for Atlanta bookkeeping hires

Staff Financial Group is a finance and accounting recruiting and staffing firm based in metro Atlanta, in business since 2005 and powered by NorthPoint Search Group. It mostly serves companies across Atlanta and the Southeast and also runs remote searches nationwide. Outside Georgia, it has placed finance and accounting people in Charlotte, Nashville, Tampa, and other Southeast markets.

Metro Atlanta is not one bookkeeping market. A distributor in Norcross, a family office in Buckhead, and a homebuilder in Cumming all need a bookkeeper, but they need different ones. The guides below are built from Staff Financial Group's local recruiting pages for each market.

## How we handle commute and work setup

Metro Atlanta is spread out, and the drive matters to bookkeepers as much as the pay.

- **Commute fit is screened early,** so offers are not lost to the drive.
- **On-site expectations are confirmed before interviews.** Bookkeepers who handle checks, mail, and deposits often need to be in the office at least part of the week.
- **Hybrid widens the pool.** In Staff Financial Group's 2026 Salary Guide, 84% of 736 candidates said they would work hybrid, 65% would work fully in the office, and only 9% will only work remote.
