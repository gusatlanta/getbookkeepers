## Hiring a bookkeeper in a commuter metro

Metro Atlanta is spread out, and the drive matters to bookkeepers as much as the pay. A few things we do on every local search:

- **Screen for commute fit early.** Where a candidate lives relative to your office is a question in the screening, so offers are not lost to the drive. On the GA-400 corridor, for example, Alpharetta employers often draw candidates from Roswell, Johns Creek, Cumming, and Milton.
- **Confirm on-site expectations before interviews.** Bookkeepers who handle checks, mail, and deposits often need to be on site at least part of the week. We confirm each candidate's in-office expectations up front so there are no surprises at offer time.
- **Consider remote when the books allow it.** If your systems are in the cloud and the work is mostly digital, a remote bookkeeper widens the pool. Tell us on the form, and we screen for remote experience.
