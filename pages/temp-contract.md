## When a temp or contract bookkeeper is the right call

A temp or contract bookkeeper makes sense when the work is real but you are not ready to add someone to your payroll. Common reasons employers call us:

- **Month-end close support.** Reconciliations, accruals, and journal entries are piling up and the close keeps slipping.
- **Payroll and AP coverage.** Your bookkeeper or AP clerk is out on leave, or just resigned, and vendors and employees still need to be paid on time.
- **A backlog or cleanup.** Months of uncategorized transactions or unreconciled accounts that need a focused pair of hands.
- **Audit preparation.** Pulling schedules and support together before your CPA or auditors arrive.
- **Busy season and growth spurts.** Extra volume for a few weeks or months.

Temp usually means a short assignment with a known end date. Contract usually means a longer engagement with a defined scope, sometimes several months. Both work the same way on billing.

## How to write the job so the right temp shows up

A clear brief gets you a better shortlist. Include:

- **The exact work.** "Reconcile 6 bank accounts and 2 credit cards monthly" beats "help with books."
- **Your systems.** QuickBooks, NetSuite, Sage, or something else, plus any bill pay or expense tool.
- **Volume.** Invoices per week, number of entities, number of employees on payroll.
- **Hours and length.** Full time or part time, and how many weeks you expect.
- **On site or remote.** And if on site, where.

## Like the bookkeeper? Keep them

If a temp or contract bookkeeper turns out to be the right long-term fit, you can convert them to your own payroll with a simple conversion agreement. If you already expect that outcome, start with [Temp-to-Hire](/temp-to-hire-bookkeepers/).

## Frequently asked questions

### How quickly can a temp bookkeeper start?

The candidates we market for temp work have flagged themselves as available now, with no notice period. Staff Financial Group sends a blinded shortlist 48 to 72 hours after your hiring request; start dates depend on your interviews and onboarding.

### Who is the employer of a temp bookkeeper?

Staff Financial Group is the Employer of Record. It runs weekly payroll, handles tax withholdings, covers workers comp under its policy, and issues the W-2. You approve hours and receive one invoice.

### Can a contract bookkeeper work remotely?

Yes, if your job allows it. Tell us on the form whether the work is on site, hybrid, or remote, and we screen for that setup.

### What if the temp bookkeeper is not a fit?

Tell your recruiter right away, and they will walk through your options with you.
