## Why a bookkeeping hire is worth doing right

Your bookkeeper touches every dollar that comes in and goes out. A weak hire shows up as late closes, missed vendor payments, and cleanup work for your CPA. A strong one gives you clean numbers every month. GetBookkeepers is run by [Staff Financial Group](https://www.stafffinancial.com/), a recruiting and staffing firm that works only in finance and accounting, so the people screening your candidates know the difference between data entry and owning a full set of books.

## What we handle for Temp, Contract, and Payrolling

When Staff Financial Group is the Employer of Record, the bookkeeper works for you and we handle the employment side:

- **Tax withholdings:** federal, state, and FICA.
- **Workers comp and insurance** under Staff Financial Group's policy.
- **W-2** at year-end.
- **One invoice** to you, instead of a new employee on your own payroll.

Direct Hire placements join your payroll from day one and are backed by Staff Financial Group's replacement guarantee.

## Frequently asked questions

### How fast can I get bookkeeper candidates?

Staff Financial Group delivers a blinded shortlist of screened candidates 48 to 72 hours after it receives your hiring request through the Get Started form.

### Can I start with a temp bookkeeper and hire them later?

Yes. Temp-to-Hire lets you see the work first. We are the Employer of Record during the contract period, and when you want to bring the bookkeeper onto your payroll, you convert them with a simple conversion agreement.

### Who handles payroll and taxes for a temp bookkeeper?

We do. As Employer of Record, Staff Financial Group runs payroll every week, handles federal, state, and FICA withholdings, covers workers comp under its policy, and issues the W-2 at year-end. You get one invoice.

### Do you only work in Atlanta?

Staff Financial Group is based in metro Atlanta and mostly serves companies across Atlanta and the Southeast. It also handles remote searches nationwide. See [bookkeepers in Atlanta](/atlanta/) for the areas we cover most.
