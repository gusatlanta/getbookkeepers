## Signs it is time to hire a bookkeeper

- You, the owner, spend evenings and weekends entering bills and reconciling accounts.
- Your books are months behind, and you find out how the business did only at tax time.
- Customers pay late and nobody is following up.
- Payroll is getting harder: more people, more states, or more pay types.
- Your outside CPA keeps sending the books back with questions or charging for cleanup.
- A lender, investor, or buyer is asking for monthly financial statements.

## What the job covers in a small business

In a small company, one bookkeeper usually does everything that a larger company splits across AP, AR, payroll, and a staff accountant. That is a [full-charge bookkeeper](/roles/full-charge-bookkeeper/):

- Paying bills and managing vendors
- Invoicing customers and collecting payments
- Running payroll or working with the payroll provider
- Reconciling bank and credit card accounts every month
- Closing the month and producing a profit and loss statement and balance sheet
- Handing clean books to the outside CPA for taxes

## Working with your outside CPA

Hiring a bookkeeper does not usually replace your CPA. The bookkeeper keeps the books every day and closes each month; the CPA handles tax returns, tax planning, and year-end adjustments. A good small business bookkeeper knows what the CPA will need and has it ready. Ask candidates how they worked with an outside accountant before.

## Skills to screen for

- **Your accounting system.** In Staff Financial Group's [2026 Salary Guide](/bookkeeper-salary/), QuickBooks was the system candidates named most (60% of 820). If you use it, ask which version and how they used it day to day.
- **Owning the close.** Ask them to walk through a month-end close they did alone.
- **Payroll.** Even with an outside payroll provider, someone has to enter hours, changes, and new hires correctly.
- **Judgment.** In a small business there is no one down the hall to ask. Ask about a time they caught a problem on their own.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can a part-time bookkeeper work for a small business?

Yes, many small businesses start part-time. Say so on the Get Started form and your recruiter will look for candidates who want that schedule.

### Should my first bookkeeper be full-charge?

Usually, yes. In a small business there is no one else to handle payables, receivables, payroll, and the close, so look for someone who has done all of them.

### Can someone clean up books that are behind?

Yes. A contract bookkeeper can catch up and reconcile your books first, and then you can decide on a longer-term hire.
