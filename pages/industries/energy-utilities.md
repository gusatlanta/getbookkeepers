## What makes energy and utilities bookkeeping different

Energy companies, utilities, and the contractors that serve them spend heavily on equipment, materials, and field labor. Costs are often tracked by work order or job, assets are large and long-lived, and field crews may work odd hours. A bookkeeper needs to keep costs on the right work order and keep the fixed asset records in step with what is in the field.

## What the job usually covers

- **Field AP.** Coding materials, equipment rental, and subcontractor bills to the right work order or job.
- **Fixed assets.** Recording equipment and vehicle purchases and keeping the asset list current.
- **Fleet costs.** Tracking fuel and maintenance.
- **Field payroll.** Overtime, shift work, and crews moving between jobs.
- **Customer billing** for service and installation work.

## Skills to screen for

- **Work orders.** Ask how they coded field costs to work orders or jobs.
- **Fixed assets.** Ask whether they kept the asset register.
- **Payroll.** Ask about overtime and crews on multiple jobs.
- **Your system.** Put your accounting and field service software in the questionnaire.

## Screening questions to use

1. "How did you make sure a field invoice was charged to the right work order?"
2. "What did you do when a vehicle was sold or retired?"
3. "How did you handle payroll for a crew that worked on three jobs in one week?"
