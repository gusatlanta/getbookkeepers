## What makes healthcare bookkeeping different

In most businesses, a sale creates one invoice to one customer. In healthcare, one visit can be paid by an insurer, a secondary insurer, and the patient, each in a different amount and at a different time. The practice rarely gets its full billed charge; the payer contract sets what it will actually pay. A practice bookkeeper or AR specialist needs to understand that flow.

## What the job usually covers

- **Payment posting.** Posting insurance payments from explanations of benefits (EOBs) and electronic remittance advice (ERAs), plus patient payments, to the right visits.
- **Contractual adjustments.** Writing off the difference between the billed charge and the payer's allowed amount, so receivables show what the practice can really collect.
- **Insurance and patient AR.** Following up on unpaid or denied claims and on patient balances.
- **Reconciling deposits.** Matching bank deposits and lockbox or card batches to posted payments.
- **The rest of the books.** Payables, payroll for clinical and front-office staff, and the monthly close, often done by one [full-charge bookkeeper](/roles/full-charge-bookkeeper/) in a smaller practice.

## Privacy

Patient payment and billing records are protected health information under HIPAA. A healthcare bookkeeper should already be used to handling it carefully, with access limited to what the job needs.

## Skills to screen for

- **Practice management or billing system.** Ask which system they posted payments in. Put yours in the screening questionnaire.
- **Posting accuracy.** Ask how they handled a payment that did not match the expected amount.
- **Denials and follow-up.** Ask how they tracked and worked unpaid claims.
- **Contractual adjustments.** Ask them to explain one in their own words.

## Screening questions to use

1. "Walk us through posting an insurance payment from an EOB."
2. "How did you reconcile daily deposits to posted payments?"
3. "What was your process for claims unpaid after 30, 60, and 90 days?"
4. "How did you handle patient balance questions?"

## Frequently asked questions

### Can a contract bookkeeper help during a practice acquisition or system change?

Yes. Staff Financial Group's Roswell page notes contract help is common for practice acquisitions, system changes, and year-end catch-up.

### Do I need a healthcare-specific bookkeeper?

For posting and insurance AR, healthcare experience saves a lot of training. For payables and the general ledger, a strong bookkeeper from another industry can learn quickly.

### Is medical billing the same as bookkeeping?

Not exactly. Billing and coding create and submit claims. Bookkeeping posts payments, records adjustments, and keeps the practice's books. In a small practice, one person sometimes oversees both.
