## What makes retail and consumer bookkeeping different

A store or restaurant records sales in a point-of-sale system, gets paid mostly by card, and deposits cash. Those three numbers rarely match on the same day, so a bookkeeper reconciles them daily or weekly. Sales tax is collected on most sales, and in Georgia the combined state and local rate depends on where the sale happens. Inventory and gift cards add more to track.

## What the job usually covers

- **Daily sales reconciliation.** Matching point-of-sale reports to card settlements and cash deposits.
- **Sales tax.** Recording tax collected and preparing returns.
- **Inventory and cost of sales**, from purchase to sale.
- **Gift cards** and store credit, which are liabilities until used.
- **Hourly and tipped payroll** for store and restaurant staff.
- **Multi-location reporting** for chains.

## Skills to screen for

- **Point-of-sale system.** Put yours in the screening questionnaire.
- **Daily reconciliation.** Ask how they matched sales to deposits.
- **Sales tax.** Ask which returns they prepared.
- **Payroll.** For restaurants, ask about tipped employees.

## Screening questions to use

1. "Card deposits are short compared with the point-of-sale report. Where do you look?"
2. "How do you record a gift card sale, and what happens when it is used?"
3. "How did you track sales tax for more than one location?"
