## What makes professional services bookkeeping different

A professional services firm earns revenue when its people do billable work, and it gets paid when clients pay the invoices. In between are unbilled time, retainers, and reimbursable expenses. A bookkeeper here spends a lot of time making sure every billable hour and expense ends up on an invoice, and every invoice gets paid.

## What the job usually covers

- **Time and billing.** Turning approved time and expenses into client invoices, by engagement.
- **Retainers.** Recording client deposits and applying them to invoices.
- **Reimbursable expenses.** Tracking costs that are billed back to clients.
- **Collections.** Following up on past-due invoices.
- **Multi-office reporting** for firms with more than one location.
- **Partner or owner draws**, coordinated with the outside CPA.

## Skills to screen for

- **Your time and billing system.** List it in the screening questionnaire.
- **Billing cycle.** Ask how they ran month-end billing and who approved invoices.
- **Collections.** Ask how they followed up on past-due clients.
- **Retainers.** Ask how they applied a retainer to an invoice.

## Screening questions to use

1. "Walk us through month-end billing at your last firm."
2. "How did you make sure reimbursable expenses were billed to clients?"
3. "How did you handle a client who was 90 days past due?"
