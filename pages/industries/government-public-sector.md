## What makes government and public sector bookkeeping different

State and local governments keep their books by fund under standards from the Governmental Accounting Standards Board (GASB), and spending is controlled by an approved budget. Purchase orders, encumbrances, and procurement rules shape how AP works. Companies that hold government contracts have their own rules about tracking costs to each contract.

## What the job usually covers

- **Fund accounting.** Recording each transaction in the right fund.
- **Budgets and encumbrances.** Checking spending against the budget and recording commitments when purchase orders are issued.
- **Purchase orders and AP** under procurement rules.
- **Grant compliance** for federal and state money.
- **Contract cost tracking** at government contractors, charging time and costs to the right contract.

## Skills to screen for

- **Fund accounting.** Ask which funds they worked in and what reports they prepared.
- **Encumbrances.** Ask how a purchase order affected the budget.
- **Contracts.** For contractors, ask how they charged time and costs to contracts.
- **Grants.** Ask what grant reporting they supported.

## Screening questions to use

1. "What happens in the books when a purchase order is issued?"
2. "How did you make sure a cost was charged to the right fund or contract?"
3. "What grant or budget reports did you prepare?"
