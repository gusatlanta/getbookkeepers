## Why industry experience matters for a bookkeeper

The basics are the same everywhere: record transactions, pay bills, bill customers, reconcile accounts, close the month. What changes by industry is where the money gets complicated. That is where a bookkeeper without the right background slows down or makes expensive mistakes.

| Industry | Where the complexity is | Guide |
|---|---|---|
| Accounting and CPA firms | Many client books at once, deadlines, payroll and sales tax for clients | [Accounting firms](/industries/accounting-firms/) |
| Small business | One person doing everything, cash flow, working with the outside CPA | [Small business](/industries/small-business/) |
| Healthcare | Insurance and patient receivables, contractual adjustments, privacy | [Healthcare](/industries/healthcare/) |
| Legal | Client trust accounts, three-way reconciliation, time-based billing | [Legal](/industries/legal/) |
| Construction | Job costing, retainage, pay applications, work-in-progress schedules | [Construction](/industries/construction/) |
