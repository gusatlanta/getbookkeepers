## Why industry experience matters for a bookkeeper

The basics are the same everywhere: record transactions, pay bills, bill customers, reconcile accounts, close the month. What changes by industry is where the money gets complicated. That is where a bookkeeper without the right background slows down or makes expensive mistakes.

| Industry | Where the complexity is | Guide |
|---|---|---|
| Accounting and CPA firms | Many client books at once, deadlines, payroll and sales tax for clients | [Accounting firms](/industries/accounting-firms/) |
| Small business | One person doing everything, cash flow, working with the outside CPA | [Small business](/industries/small-business/) |
| Healthcare | Insurance and patient receivables, contractual adjustments, privacy | [Healthcare](/industries/healthcare/) |
| Legal | Client trust accounts, three-way reconciliation, time-based billing | [Legal](/industries/legal/) |
| Construction | Job costing, retainage, pay applications, work-in-progress schedules | [Construction](/industries/construction/) |
| Manufacturing | Inventory, three-way match, cost of goods sold, shift payroll | [Manufacturing](/industries/manufacturing/) |
| Distribution and logistics | High-volume AP and AR, freight bills, deductions, collections | [Distribution and logistics](/industries/distribution-logistics/) |
| Technology and SaaS | Subscription billing, deferred revenue, a fast close, audit readiness | [Technology and SaaS](/industries/technology-saas/) |
| Private equity and family offices | Many entities, capital calls, monthly reporting to owners | [Private equity](/industries/private-equity/) |
| Real estate | Tenant ledgers, security deposits, owner statements, property books | [Real estate](/industries/real-estate/) |
| Professional services | Time and billing, retainers, reimbursable expenses, collections | [Professional services](/industries/professional-services/) |
| Nonprofit and education | Donor restrictions, grants, functional expenses, audits | [Nonprofit and education](/industries/nonprofit-education/) |
| Government and public sector | Fund accounting, budgets and encumbrances, contract costs | [Government and public sector](/industries/government-public-sector/) |
| Financial services | Account reconciliations, commissions, controls, audit support | [Financial services](/industries/financial-services/) |
| Retail and consumer | Daily sales to deposits, sales tax, inventory, gift cards | [Retail and consumer](/industries/retail-consumer/) |
| Energy and utilities | Field costs by work order, fixed assets, field payroll | [Energy and utilities](/industries/energy-utilities/) |
