## Direct Hire, explained

In a Direct Hire search, the bookkeeper you choose joins your payroll as your own employee from day one. Staff Financial Group does the recruiting and screening, and you pay one fee, quoted up front before the search starts. Every Direct Hire placement is backed by Staff Financial Group's replacement guarantee: if the person leaves within the guarantee period, we replace them at no additional fee.

Direct Hire is the right choice when:

- You know what the role needs and the workload is steady.
- You want the bookkeeper to grow with the company, maybe into an accounting manager or controller seat later.
- You would rather pay one fee than an hourly bill rate.

## How a Direct Hire bookkeeper search works

1. **Discovery call.** We learn the role, your systems, your team, your CPA relationship, and your timeline. Not just the job description.
2. **Screening questionnaire.** We build it with you. If month-end close in NetSuite or multi-entity QuickBooks is the make-or-break skill, the questions go straight at it.
3. **Targeted search.** We search our active network and reach bookkeepers who are not browsing job boards.
4. **Blinded shortlist in 48 to 72 hours.** Each profile shows skills, experience, and the questionnaire answers. Every candidate is invited to record an optional video intro.
5. **Interviews and offer.** We coordinate scheduling, collect feedback, and help you land the offer.

## What to screen for in a bookkeeper

Good questionnaire questions are specific. A few that work well:

- **"Walk us through your last month-end close. What did you own, and how many days did it take?"** Separates people who ran the close from people who supported it.
- **"Which accounting systems have you used daily, and for how long?"** Daily use is different from "familiar with."
- **"How many bank, card, and loan accounts did you reconcile each month?"** Gives you a sense of volume.
- **"Have you handled payroll or sales tax filings yourself?"** Many small companies need both.
- **"Tell us about a reconciliation that would not tie out. How did you find the problem?"** Shows how they think.

## Set the salary with real numbers

Offers get turned down when the pay is off. In Staff Financial Group's [2026 Salary Guide](https://www.stafffinancial.com/salary-guide.html), the median desired base salary was $70K for bookkeepers, $67K for AP, AR, and billing roles, and $70K for payroll roles, from 805 candidates between January and October 2026. Use it as a starting point and adjust for the scope of your role.
