## From Tech Square startups to Peachtree Street

Around Tech Square, next to Georgia Tech, venture-funded companies often need their first real bookkeeper or finance lead to get the books ready for investors. A few blocks south, large headquarters run deep corporate accounting departments with formal close calendars.

Midtown's nonprofits, universities, and arts organizations add fund accounting and grant reporting. See the [nonprofit and education guide](/industries/nonprofit-education/). Law, consulting, and agency firms need time-based client billing; see the [professional services guide](/industries/professional-services/).

## Transit, parking, and hybrid norms

Midtown is served by MARTA's North Avenue, Midtown, and Arts Center stations, so employers can recruit people who commute by rail from across the system. Staff Financial Group notes that parking costs make transit and hybrid schedules a real factor in offers.

## Ways to hire a bookkeeper in Midtown

A startup that is not ready for a full-time hire can start with [Temp or Contract](/temp-contract-bookkeepers/) help. Nonprofits that want to see grant work first can use [Temp-to-Hire](/temp-to-hire-bookkeepers/). Headquarters filling a long-term seat use [Direct Hire](/direct-hire-bookkeepers/). Every search starts with a screening questionnaire built with you, and a blinded shortlist comes back in 48 to 72 hours.
