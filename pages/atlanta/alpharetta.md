## Where the bookkeeping jobs are in Alpharetta

Alpharetta calls itself the Technology City of the South. The office parks along Windward Parkway and North Point Parkway are full of software, payments, and data companies. Their accounting teams deal with subscription revenue, more than one legal entity, and getting ready for an outside audit, so a bookkeeper there usually supports a controller and works to a firm close calendar.

Downtown Alpharetta and Avalon look different. Smaller headquarters and professional firms often want one strong generalist who can own the monthly close and keep an eye on payroll. That is the classic [full-charge bookkeeper](/roles/full-charge-bookkeeper/) job.

## Bookkeeping jobs Alpharetta employers hire for

- **[AP and AR clerks](/roles/accounts-payable-and-receivable-clerks/)** for growth companies with steady invoice volume and payments to reconcile.
- **[Full-charge bookkeepers](/roles/full-charge-bookkeeper/)** for downtown and Avalon offices that need one person to own the books.
- **[Staff accountants](/roles/staff-accountant/)** when a bookkeeper is ready to move up into reconciliations and the close.
- **[Payroll specialists](/roles/payroll-specialist/)** for employers that run payroll in-house.
