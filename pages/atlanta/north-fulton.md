## Alpharetta: growth companies and regional offices

Office parks along Windward Parkway and North Point Parkway hold software, payments, and data companies whose accounting teams handle subscription revenue, multi-entity consolidations, and audit readiness for outside investors. Downtown Alpharetta and Avalon add smaller headquarters and professional firms that want one strong generalist who can own the close and payroll oversight.

**What that means for a bookkeeping hire:** in a growth company, a bookkeeper or AP and AR specialist often supports a controller and needs to keep up with investor-driven close deadlines and deferred revenue schedules. In a smaller office, a [full-charge bookkeeper](/roles/full-charge-bookkeeper/) may be the whole accounting function.

## Roswell: practices, professional firms, and Main Street

Roswell's employers range from healthcare and medical practices near Holcomb Bridge Road to professional firms in the Mansell Road office corridor and small businesses around Historic Roswell. Around Canton Street and the historic district, restaurants, retailers, and service businesses often need an experienced bookkeeper or part-time help rather than a full department. Staff Financial Group's Roswell page lists full-charge bookkeepers and payroll administrators among the roles it fills there, and notes that contract help is common for practice acquisitions, system changes, and year-end catch-up.

See the [healthcare bookkeeping guide](/industries/healthcare/) and the [small business guide](/industries/small-business/) for what those employers should screen for.

## Johns Creek: technology, life sciences, and healthcare

The Technology Parkway office cluster, the hospital, and medical offices give Johns Creek a mix that leans toward technology, life sciences, and healthcare. Healthcare employers around the hospital campus and along Medlock Bridge and State Bridge Roads need revenue cycle and practice finance staff. Johns Creek is not on GA-400; candidates reach it by Medlock Bridge, State Bridge, and McGinnis Ferry Roads, which helps employers attract people from Duluth, Suwanee, Alpharetta, and Cumming who want to avoid the interstate.

## Cumming and Forsyth County: first in-house hires

Cumming is the seat of Forsyth County, and its finance hiring follows growth: homebuilders, contractors, medical groups, and service companies adding their first or second accounting hire. Homebuilders, trade contractors, and property managers need job costing, draw management, retainage tracking, and work-in-progress schedules. Many are owner-led companies moving from an outside bookkeeper to an in-house accounting function for the first time. Healthcare, dental, and veterinary groups, plus retail and service businesses, often want one person who can run payroll, payables, and the monthly close.

Staff Financial Group's Cumming page lists full-charge bookkeepers, payroll administrators, and AP and AR staff among the roles it fills, and notes that [Temp-to-Hire](/temp-to-hire-bookkeepers/) is a good fit for owners who want to see how someone works before committing. For construction employers, see the [construction bookkeeping guide](/industries/construction/).

## Commute and work setup on the GA-400 corridor

- Most Alpharetta finance roles draw from Roswell, Johns Creek, Cumming, and Milton, where candidates can reach the office without crossing I-285.
- Many Forsyth residents commute south on GA-400 every day, so a local role is a strong recruiting message in Cumming. Candidates there come from Cumming, Suwanee, Buford, Dawsonville, and north Alpharetta, and roles are mostly on site.
- Staff Financial Group screens for commute fit early so offers are not lost to the drive.
