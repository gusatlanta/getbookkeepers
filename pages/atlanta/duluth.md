## Distribution, international offices, and the Gas South District

Warehouses and distribution centers along I-85 and Peachtree Industrial need AP and AR people who are comfortable with high invoice volume, freight, and inventory reconciliations. U.S. subsidiaries of overseas parents need reporting back to a foreign headquarters and careful intercompany entries. The Gas South District, with its arena and convention center, adds event and hospitality bookkeeping.

Across all of these, bilingual English and Korean finance candidates are a frequent request, and Staff Financial Group can make language a screening requirement.
