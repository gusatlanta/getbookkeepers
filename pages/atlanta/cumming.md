## Growth companies in Forsyth County

Residential growth in Forsyth County has brought homebuilders, trade contractors, and property managers. Their books need job costing, draw management, retainage tracking, and work-in-progress schedules. The [construction bookkeeping guide](/industries/construction/) explains each of those.

Many of these are owner-led companies moving from an outside bookkeeper to an in-house person for the first time. Dental, veterinary, and healthcare groups, plus retail and service businesses around the GA-400 exits and The Collection at Forsyth, add more demand. They often want one capable person who can run payroll, payables, and the monthly close.
