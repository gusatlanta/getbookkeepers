## Cumberland: shared services at scale

The Cumberland district at the I-75 and I-285 interchange is one of metro Atlanta's largest office markets, with corporate headquarters, shared-services centers, and regional finance hubs around the Galleria and Circle 75 Parkway. Finance teams here run on scale: intercompany accounting, lease accounting, and large AP operations. Employers usually want specialists who have worked inside a bigger close calendar rather than generalists. Growth around The Battery Atlanta and Truist Park has added mixed-use and hospitality employers whose needs include revenue audit and property-level accounting.

**What that means for a bookkeeping hire:** in a shared-services AP team, a clerk's speed, accuracy, and comfort with approval workflows matter more than breadth. Ask about weekly invoice volume, three-way match, and vendor master controls. See the [AP and AR clerk guide](/roles/accounts-payable-and-receivable-clerks/).

## Marietta: family companies, manufacturers, and public sector

As the Cobb County seat, Marietta mixes public-sector finance, aerospace and manufacturing suppliers, healthcare groups, and a large base of family-owned companies around the Square and East Cobb. Near Dobbins Air Reserve Base and along I-75, aerospace and defense suppliers need people who understand job costing and contract compliance. Government and education employers need governmental and fund accounting experience. Many family companies are second- or third-generation businesses where one finance person owns the books and manages the outside CPA relationship.

Staff Financial Group's Marietta page lists staff accountants and bookkeepers moving up, cost accountants, and payroll specialists among the roles it fills. Recent graduates from the Kennesaw State University Marietta campus often fill entry roles, and early-career candidates can be included in a search.

**What that means for a bookkeeping hire:** for a family company, look for a [full-charge bookkeeper](/roles/full-charge-bookkeeper/) who can own the close and work well with the outside CPA. For a manufacturer or contractor, screen for job costing. See the [construction bookkeeping guide](/industries/construction/).

## Commute and work setup

- Cumberland candidates mostly come from Smyrna, Vinings, Marietta, Kennesaw, and the west side of Sandy Springs. Traffic at the interchange shapes who will accept an offer, and many Cumberland employers offer hybrid schedules to offset the commute.
- Marietta candidates come from Kennesaw, Acworth, Smyrna, Powder Springs, and East Cobb. East Cobb residents often prefer roles that avoid the I-75 merge, while west-side candidates reach the Square easily. Most Marietta finance roles are on site or lightly hybrid.
