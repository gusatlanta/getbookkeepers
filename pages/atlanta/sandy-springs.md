## Perimeter Center headquarters and the Medical Center

The towers near the GA-400 and I-285 interchange hold national and regional headquarters, insurance and financial services operations, and franchise systems. Their finance teams report across many locations, and franchise systems need royalty accounting. See the [financial services guide](/industries/financial-services/).

The Medical Center area brings hospital and physician group finance. City Springs and the Roswell Road corridor add smaller professional firms and real estate businesses that need hands-on bookkeeping. See the [real estate guide](/industries/real-estate/).

## MARTA Red Line and the 400/285 interchange

The MARTA Red Line serves the area at the Medical Center, Dunwoody, Sandy Springs, and North Springs stations, so employers can recruit from Midtown and Buckhead without a car commute. The 400/285 interchange is congested, so many Perimeter employers offer hybrid schedules. Staff Financial Group covers Dunwoody as part of Perimeter.
