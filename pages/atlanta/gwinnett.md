## Norcross: manufacturing, wholesale, and logistics

Between Jimmy Carter Boulevard, Peachtree Industrial Boulevard, and the Technology Parkway area, Norcross has a dense mix of manufacturers, wholesalers, and logistics operators. Their accounting needs lean toward standard costing, inventory reconciliations, freight accruals, and month-end variance analysis. Next door, Peachtree Corners and Technology Park add engineering and software firms with leaner finance teams, where one hire often covers both the general ledger and reporting.

Norcross employers ask Staff Financial Group for AP and AR leads who can handle high transaction volume, credit and collections specialists, and payroll staff familiar with hourly and multi-shift workforces. Bilingual English and Spanish candidates are frequently requested for plant-facing roles, and that can be a screening requirement.

## Duluth: distribution and international offices

Duluth's position on I-85 makes it a base for distributors, logistics operators, and U.S. offices of international companies, with a large Korean-American business community along Pleasant Hill Road and Satellite Boulevard. Warehouses and distribution centers need accounting people comfortable with high invoice volume, freight and landed cost, and inventory reconciliations. U.S. subsidiaries of overseas parents need reporting to a foreign headquarters and intercompany accounting.

Staff Financial Group's Duluth page lists AP and AR specialists for high-volume operations, payroll specialists, and bilingual English and Korean staff among the roles it fills. Contract help is common for audit preparation and parent-company reporting deadlines.

## What to screen for in a Gwinnett bookkeeping hire

- **Volume.** Ask how many invoices a week the candidate processed and how many vendors they managed. See the [AP and AR clerk guide](/roles/accounts-payable-and-receivable-clerks/).
- **Inventory and freight.** Ask whether they reconciled inventory, booked freight accruals, or matched landed cost.
- **Hourly payroll.** For plant and warehouse payroll, ask about shifts, overtime, and timekeeping systems. See the [payroll specialist guide](/roles/payroll-specialist/).
- **Your ERP.** Put your system in the screening questionnaire. In the 2026 Salary Guide, the most common systems candidates named were QuickBooks (60%), Oracle (43%), SAP (41%), Microsoft Dynamics (30%), and NetSuite (30%).
- **Language.** If English and Spanish, or English and Korean, matters for the job, make it a screening requirement.

## Commute and schedules

- Norcross draws candidates from Duluth, Lilburn, Doraville, Tucker, and Lawrenceville. Plant-adjacent finance roles tend to be fully on site, with earlier start times than downtown offices, and Staff Financial Group confirms schedule fit during screening.
- Duluth draws from Lawrenceville, Suwanee, Norcross, Johns Creek, and Buford. Pleasant Hill Road and the I-85 interchanges can be slow at rush hour, so candidates weigh the exact office location. Most roles are on site.

## Which hire type fits

High-volume AP and AR coverage during a system conversion or a backlog is a natural [contract](/temp-contract-bookkeepers/) job, with Staff Financial Group as Employer of Record. Steady AP, AR, and payroll seats are usually [Direct Hire](/direct-hire-bookkeepers/).
