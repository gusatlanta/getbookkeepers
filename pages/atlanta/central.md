## Buckhead: family offices, investment firms, and law firms

Buckhead's towers along Peachtree Road are home to investment managers, private equity firms, family offices, real estate sponsors, and professional firms. Between Lenox Square, Phipps Plaza, and Buckhead Village, many employers are small teams managing large balance sheets. A family office controller may cover entity bookkeeping, tax coordination, and personal financial statements for principals. Real estate owners need property and partnership accounting, and law and advisory firms need partner-level billing and trust accounting.

**What that means for a bookkeeping hire:** a Buckhead bookkeeper often keeps books for many related entities at once and handles sensitive information. Screen for multi-entity experience and discretion. Many Buckhead searches are confidential replacements, and Staff Financial Group runs them that way. For law firms, see the [legal bookkeeping guide](/industries/legal/) on trust accounting.

## Midtown: headquarters, startups, nonprofits, and firms

Midtown packs corporate headquarters, Tech Square startups, universities, arts institutions, and professional firms along Peachtree and West Peachtree Streets. Venture-funded companies near Georgia Tech often need their first finance hire. Midtown's nonprofits, universities, and arts organizations add fund accounting and grant reporting work, and its law, consulting, and agency firms need engagement accounting and billing.

**What that means for a bookkeeping hire:** a nonprofit bookkeeper should know restricted versus unrestricted funds and grant tracking. A firm bookkeeper should know time-based billing and client receivables. Ask about both directly in your screening questionnaire.

## Sandy Springs and Perimeter Center: franchises and hospitals

Sandy Springs shares Perimeter Center with Dunwoody, one of the region's biggest concentrations of corporate offices, and is home to the hospital cluster along Peachtree Dunwoody Road known as Pill Hill. Perimeter headquarters and franchise systems run multi-location reporting and franchise or royalty accounting. The Medical Center area brings hospital and physician group finance, including revenue cycle work. City Springs and the Roswell Road corridor add smaller professional firms and real estate businesses that need hands-on accounting help.

For hospital and practice bookkeeping, see the [healthcare bookkeeping guide](/industries/healthcare/).

## Commute: MARTA and the 400/285 interchange

- Buckhead is reachable by the MARTA Red Line at the Buckhead and Lenox stations and by GA-400, which widens the candidate pool to Brookhaven, Sandy Springs, Midtown, and North Fulton. Investment and family office roles are more often fully in the office than corporate roles elsewhere in the metro.
- Midtown is served by the MARTA North Avenue, Midtown, and Arts Center stations. Parking costs make transit and hybrid schedules a real factor in offers, and Staff Financial Group covers them in screening.
- In Sandy Springs, the Red Line stops at Medical Center, Dunwoody, Sandy Springs, and North Springs. The 400/285 interchange is congested, so many Perimeter employers offer hybrid schedules.
