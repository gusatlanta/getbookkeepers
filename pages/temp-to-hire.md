## Why Temp-to-Hire works well for bookkeepers

A resume and an interview tell you a lot, but bookkeeping is easiest to judge by watching it happen. In a few weeks you will know whether a bookkeeper reconciles on time, asks good questions, keeps clean support for entries, and works well with your team and your CPA. Temp-to-Hire gives you that view before you commit.

It is a good fit when:

- You are hiring your **first in-house bookkeeper** and are not sure exactly what the role needs yet.
- You are moving from an **outside bookkeeping service** to someone in-house.
- Your last hire **did not work out** and you want more certainty this time.
- The role is **new or growing**, and you want to see the real workload before setting the salary.

## How it works

1. **Send the job.** On the Get Started form, choose "Contract-to-Hire" as the hire type.
2. **Screen and interview.** We build a screening questionnaire with you, and every resume comes with the candidate's answers. Say on the form that you plan to convert, so the screening covers whether each candidate wants a Direct Hire role.
3. **Contract period.** The bookkeeper works for you while Staff Financial Group is the Employer of Record: weekly payroll, tax withholdings, workers comp, and one invoice at your agreed bill rate.
4. **Convert.** When you are ready, you bring the bookkeeper onto your payroll with a simple conversion agreement.

## Make the trial period count

Plan the contract period around the work that matters most, so the decision is easy:

- **Include at least one month-end close.** It is where most bookkeeping problems show up.
- **Write down what "good" looks like.** For example: bank and card reconciliations done by the fifth business day, AP aging reviewed weekly, no unexplained balances in suspense accounts.
- **Check in at the halfway point.** Share feedback with the bookkeeper and with your recruiter, so there are no surprises at conversion time.
- **Talk about pay early.** Know the salary you will offer at conversion before the trial starts. Staff Financial Group's [2026 Salary Guide](https://www.stafffinancial.com/salary-guide.html) can help.

## Frequently asked questions

### How long is a typical Temp-to-Hire period?

You set it with your recruiter when the job starts. Plan for long enough to see at least one full month-end close.

### How do I convert a temp bookkeeper to my own employee?

You sign a simple conversion agreement with Staff Financial Group, and the bookkeeper moves to your payroll as your employee.

### Who pays the bookkeeper during the trial?

Staff Financial Group does, as the Employer of Record. It runs weekly payroll and tax withholdings, and you pay one invoice at your agreed bill rate.

### What if I decide not to convert?

Tell your recruiter, and they will walk through your options with you.
