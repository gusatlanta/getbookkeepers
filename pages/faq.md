## Frequently asked questions

### Who runs GetBookkeepers?

GetBookkeepers is a service of Staff Financial Group, a finance and accounting recruiting and staffing firm in metro Atlanta since 2005, powered by NorthPoint Search Group. Its main site is [stafffinancial.com](https://www.stafffinancial.com/).

### How fast will I see bookkeeper candidates?

Staff Financial Group delivers a blinded shortlist of screened candidates 48 to 72 hours after it receives your hiring request.

### What does a blinded shortlist mean?

Each profile shows the candidate's skills, experience, title, and location, without their name, employer, or personal details. That protects candidates while you review. When you want to meet someone, we make the introduction.

### What is a screening questionnaire?

For each job, we build a short questionnaire with you about the skills that matter most for your books. Every resume we send comes with the candidate's answers, so your key questions are answered before the first call.

### Do candidates record video intros?

Every candidate is invited to record an optional video intro, so you can see how they come across before you meet.

### What is the difference between Temp, Contract, Temp-to-Hire, and Direct Hire?

Temp and Contract bookkeepers work for you while Staff Financial Group employs and payrolls them, for a short assignment or a longer engagement. Temp-to-Hire starts the same way, with the plan to convert the bookkeeper to your payroll. Direct Hire means the bookkeeper joins your payroll from day one.

### Who pays a temp or contract bookkeeper?

Staff Financial Group does. It payrolls the bookkeeper every week as Employer of Record and sends you one invoice.

### Is there a guarantee on Direct Hire placements?

Yes. Every Direct Hire placement is backed by Staff Financial Group's replacement guarantee. If the person leaves within the guarantee period, we replace them.

### What does Employer of Record mean?

Staff Financial Group is the legal employer of temp, contract, and payrolled workers. It handles weekly payroll, federal, state, and FICA withholdings, workers comp under its policy, and the W-2 at year-end. You get one invoice.

### Can you payroll a bookkeeper I already found?

Yes. That is Payrolling. Staff Financial Group employs the person you chose and handles payroll, taxes, and the W-2. See [Payrolling](/payrolling/).

### Can I convert a temp bookkeeper to my own employee?

Yes, with a simple conversion agreement.

### Which bookkeeping roles can you fill?

Bookkeepers, full-charge bookkeepers, AP and AR clerks, payroll specialists, and staff accountants, plus senior finance and accounting roles up to CFO. See [bookkeeper roles](/roles/).

### Can I browse available bookkeepers before I submit a job?

Yes. Browse [blinded profiles of available temps](https://www.stafffinancial.com/temps.html), searchable by title, skill, and location. No sign-up needed to look.

### Do you work outside Atlanta?

Staff Financial Group is based in metro Atlanta, mostly serves companies across Atlanta and the Southeast, and handles remote searches nationwide.
