## The four roles at a glance

| Role | Owns | Hire when | Median desired base* |
|---|---|---|---|
| [AP and AR clerk](/roles/accounts-payable-and-receivable-clerks/) | Paying bills, billing customers, collecting cash | Transaction volume is burying your bookkeeper or controller | $67K (AP, AR, billing) |
| [Full-charge bookkeeper](/roles/full-charge-bookkeeper/) | The whole set of books through the monthly close | You need one person to run the books end to end | $70K (bookkeeper) |
| [Payroll specialist](/roles/payroll-specialist/) | Accurate, on-time payroll, tax filings, and records | Headcount, states, or pay complexity outgrew a side task | $70K (payroll) |
| [Staff accountant](/roles/staff-accountant/) | Journal entries, reconciliations, and closes under GAAP | You need accrual accounting, reporting, and audit support | $70K (staff accountant) |

*Median desired base salary from Staff Financial Group's [2026 Salary Guide](https://www.stafffinancial.com/salary-guide.html), 805 candidates, January to October 2026. These are what candidates asked for, not offers.

## How to pick the right level

**Start with the work, not the title.** List what has to happen every week and every month: bills paid, invoices sent, payroll run, bank accounts reconciled, books closed, reports to the owner or the CPA. Then look at who does each piece today.

- **If the pain is volume** (too many invoices, too many bills), add an AP or AR clerk and let your current bookkeeper keep the close.
- **If nobody owns the close,** you need a full-charge bookkeeper.
- **If payroll keeps causing mistakes or late filings,** it is time for a payroll specialist.
- **If your CPA or lender needs accrual statements,** or you are heading into an audit, look at a staff accountant.

## Where bookkeepers fit as you grow

Small companies often start with one full-charge bookkeeper and an outside CPA. As volume grows, AP and AR clerks take over transactions, and the full-charge bookkeeper moves toward the close and reporting. When the company needs GAAP reporting, budgets, and audits, a staff accountant joins, and later an accounting manager or controller. Staff Financial Group recruits every one of those seats, from staff accountant to CFO, so the next hire is a call away.
