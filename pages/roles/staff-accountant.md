## Bookkeeper or staff accountant?

The roles overlap, and the line is not always sharp. The main difference is the accounting judgment the job needs.

| | Full-charge bookkeeper | Staff accountant |
|---|---|---|
| Usual background | Hands-on experience, sometimes a certificate or associate degree | Usually a bachelor's degree in accounting |
| Typical focus | Recording, reconciling, closing small and midsize books | Accrual entries, account analysis, GAAP reporting, audit support |
| Usually reports to | Owner, office manager, or outside CPA | Accounting manager or controller |
| When to hire | One person needs to run the books | Reporting, audits, or growth need more rigor |

If you are not sure which you need, read the [full-charge bookkeeper guide](/roles/full-charge-bookkeeper/) or pick "Not sure yet" on the Get Started form.

## What a staff accountant does

- Prepares journal entries for accruals, prepaids, deferred revenue, and fixed assets.
- Reconciles balance sheet accounts and writes up account analyses.
- Owns parts of the month-end close checklist and hits close deadlines.
- Prepares schedules and support for auditors, lenders, and the tax preparer.
- Helps with budgets, variance explanations, and ad hoc reporting.
- Often supports AP, AR, or payroll in smaller teams.

## When to hire one

- A bank, investor, or board is asking for accrual-basis or GAAP financial statements.
- You are heading into your first audit or review.
- Your controller or accounting manager is doing reconciliations instead of managing.
- You are adding entities, locations, or an ERP like NetSuite.

## Screening questions that work

1. **"Walk us through the accruals you booked each month and how you supported them."**
2. **"Which balance sheet accounts did you reconcile, and what did a finished reconciliation include?"**
3. **"What did you prepare for the auditors? What questions came back?"**
4. **"How many days did your close take, and which steps were yours?"**
5. **"Describe a variance you had to explain. What did you find?"**

## Frequently asked questions

### Can a staff accountant also do bookkeeping?

Yes, and in smaller companies they usually do. Many staff accountants handle AP, AR, and reconciliations along with their accrual and reporting work.

### Does a staff accountant need a CPA license?

Usually not. A CPA license is more common in senior accountant, accounting manager, and controller roles. If your job requires one, say so on the form and it goes into your screening questionnaire.
