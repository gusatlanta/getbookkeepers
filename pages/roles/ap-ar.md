## What an AP clerk does

Accounts payable is everything between a vendor invoice arriving and the payment clearing:

- Matching invoices to purchase orders and receiving documents (the "three-way match"), and getting approvals.
- Coding invoices to the right accounts, departments, and jobs.
- Running payment batches by check, ACH, or card, on a schedule.
- Keeping vendor records clean, including W-9s for year-end 1099s.
- Answering vendor questions and reconciling vendor statements.

## What an AR clerk does

Accounts receivable is everything between a sale and the cash landing:

- Creating and sending invoices, often from a billing system, contracts, or time records.
- Applying customer payments, including lockbox and ACH remittances.
- Following up on past-due invoices, by email and phone, with a clear escalation path.
- Keeping the AR aging accurate and flagging accounts that may not pay.
- Handling credit memos, disputes, and customer statements.

Smaller companies often combine both into one AP and AR clerk, or fold them into the [full-charge bookkeeper](/roles/full-charge-bookkeeper/) role.

## When to hire one

- Your bookkeeper or controller spends most of the week on bills and invoices instead of the close.
- Vendors are calling about late payments, or you have paid an invoice twice.
- Your AR aging keeps growing and nobody owns collections.
- You are adding a billing system, a bill pay tool, or a new ERP and need someone to run it day to day.

## Skills to screen for

| Skill | Why it matters | Ask |
|---|---|---|
| Volume | A clerk who handled 50 invoices a week is a different hire than one who handled 500 | "How many invoices did you process in a typical week?" |
| Systems | Speed comes from knowing the tools | "Which accounting and AP systems did you use every day?" |
| Controls | Duplicate and fraudulent payments are expensive | "How did you catch duplicate invoices or changed bank details?" |
| Collections | Getting paid takes persistence and tact | "Walk us through how you handled an account 60 days past due." |
| Accuracy under deadlines | Payment runs and month-end do not move | "What did you do when an approval was missing on payment day?" |

## Which hire type fits

- **[Temp or Contract](/temp-contract-bookkeepers/)** to cover a leave, a cleanup of old vendor records, or a system switch. Payroll and AP coverage is one of the most common contract requests.
- **[Temp-to-Hire](/temp-to-hire-bookkeepers/)** if the role is new and you want to see the real volume first.
- **[Direct Hire](/direct-hire-bookkeepers/)** when the volume is steady and you want the clerk to grow into a senior or lead role.

## Frequently asked questions

### Should I hire one person for AP and AR, or two?

It depends on volume. Many smaller companies combine both into one role. When invoice counts or collections work grow, splitting them gives each side a clear owner, and it also separates paying money out from bringing money in, which is a sound control.

### What systems should an AP or AR clerk know?

The ones you use every day. List your accounting system, bill pay tool, and billing system on the Get Started form, and we put those questions in your screening questionnaire.

### Can I get a temp AP clerk to cover a leave?

Yes. Temp and contract coverage for payroll and AP is a common request, and Staff Financial Group payrolls the temp as Employer of Record while they cover the work.
