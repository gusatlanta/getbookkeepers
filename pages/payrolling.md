## What payrolling means

Payrolling is for when you already have the bookkeeper. Maybe it is a former employee coming back part time, a referral from your CPA, or a contractor you want to keep on a W-2 instead of a 1099. Staff Financial Group becomes the legal employer of that person, while they do the work for you.

## What we handle as Employer of Record

- **Weekly payroll.** The bookkeeper gets paid every week.
- **Payroll taxes.** Federal, state, and FICA withholdings.
- **Workers comp and insurance.** Covered under Staff Financial Group's policy, not yours.
- **W-2 reporting.** Issued at year-end.
- **401(k) and benefits.** Every hire we payroll gets our 401(k) program, and benefits are available.
- **One invoice.** You get one invoice instead of another employee on your own payroll.

## When payrolling makes sense

- **You want someone for a few months** and do not want to add them to your own payroll.
- **You would rather not handle the HR paperwork** for one or two workers.
- **You want to try the working relationship** before you hire them yourself. When you are ready, convert them with a simple conversion agreement.
- **You found more than one person.** Payrolling works for AP clerks, payroll specialists, and staff accountants too, not only bookkeepers.

## How to start

1. Choose "Contract / Interim" on the Get Started form and note in the message that you already have the person.
2. Share the bookkeeper's name, pay rate, schedule, start date, and work location with your recruiter.
3. We onboard them as our employee and set up invoicing with you.
4. They start; we payroll them every week.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can you payroll a bookkeeper I found myself?

Yes. Staff Financial Group can act as Employer of Record for a worker you already chose, handling payroll, taxes, workers comp, and the W-2. You get one invoice.

### Can I hire a payrolled bookkeeper onto my own staff later?

Yes. Converting the bookkeeper to your own payroll is done with a simple conversion agreement.

### Is a payrolled worker a W-2 employee or a contractor?

A W-2 employee of Staff Financial Group. Staff Financial Group withholds taxes and issues the W-2 at year-end.

### Do payrolled workers get benefits?

Every hire Staff Financial Group payrolls gets its 401(k) program, and benefits are available.
